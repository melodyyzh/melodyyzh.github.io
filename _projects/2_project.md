Many nanomaterial systems of practical interest involve particles with non-spherical, convex geometries — cubes, rods, polyhedra, and more. The anisotropic nature of interactions between such particles plays a critical role in determining their self-assembly pathways and resulting structures, yet existing coarse-grained models often assume spherical symmetry.

In this project, I designed a generalized ML-IAM framework that explicitly models anisotropic interactions for nanoparticles with convex shapes. This enables the framework to be broadly applicable across a wide range of nanomaterial systems beyond the spherical PGN case.

This work is currently a manuscript in preparation.
