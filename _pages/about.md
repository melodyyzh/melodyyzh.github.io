Hi there! I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Chemical Engineering and Scientific Computing at the University of Michigan, co-advised by Prof. [Rebecca K. Lindsey](https://lindseylab.engin.umich.edu/) and Prof. [Sharon C. Glotzer](https://glotzerlab.engin.umich.edu/home/).

My research sits at the intersection of AI and scientific computing, where I develop physics-informed machine-learned interaction models (ML-IAMs) to accelerate the discovery and inverse design of self-assembling nanomaterials.

- **Current Research:** Building generalized ML interaction models for anisotropic nanoparticles that capture orientation-dependent interactions and expand the applicability of ML-IAMs to complex self-assembly processes.
- **Previous Work:** Developed the extended ChIMES framework, which incorporates physics-informed design parameters into machine-learned interaction models for nanoparticle self-assembly (read the publication [here](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/abs/10.1021/acs.jctc.5c00901)).
- **Industry & Outreach:** Beyond academia, I developed AI-enabled modeling and data infrastructure at Micron Technology and write research highlights for MRS Bulletin Materials News.

Outside of research, you can find me boxing, snowboarding, running, gaming, or behind a camera capturing landscapes and automobiles.
