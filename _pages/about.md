I am a **Ph.D. candidate** in **Chemical Engineering** and **Scientific Computing** at the **University of Michigan**, advised by Professors [Rebecca K. Lindsey](https://lindseylab.engin.umich.edu/) and [Sharon C. Glotzer](https://glotzerlab.engin.umich.edu/home/). My research develops **physics-informed machine-learned interaction models** for **coarse-grained nanomaterials**, encoding chemical and physical design parameters to enable **inverse design** and discovery of **self-assembling materials**.

I am broadly passionate about integrating **AI with scientific computing** to accelerate discovery, with interests spanning **molecular simulation**, **high-performance computing**, and **materials informatics**, including AI-enabled modeling and data infrastructure at **Micron Technology**.

I also write for **MRS Bulletin Materials News**, creating research highlights that make cutting-edge materials science accessible to broad audiences.

Outside research, I enjoy **boxing**, **snowboarding**, **running**, landscape and automotive **photography**, and gaming.
