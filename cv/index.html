Research Experience

Oct. 2022 – Present
Graduate Student Research Assistant
Lindsey Group & Glotzer Group, University of Michigan
  • Extended the machine-learned interaction model (ML-IAM), ChIMES, to describe how effective interactions between polymer-grafted nanoparticles depend on key physicochemical attributes.
  • Integrated the extended ChIMES model with digital alchemy inverse design for efficient optimization of particle attributes toward target self-assembled structures.
  • Designed a generalized ML-IAM framework that models anisotropic interactions for anisotropic nanoparticles with convex shapes, enabling broad applicability to nanomaterial systems.
  • Developed an adaptive parallel meshing algorithm for training data generation that reduces required data volume by ~100× by focusing on key features of the target potential energy surface.
  • Contributed to the development and improvement of state-of-the-art machine-learning and simulation software, including ChIMES, HOOMD-blue, and freud.
Jan. 2020 – May 2022
Undergraduate Research Assistant
Dou Group, Purdue University
  • Modified the surface of formamidinium-based perovskites using synthesized ligands to passivate defects and kinetically trap the photovoltaic crystal phase.
  • Incorporated amine-thiol chemistry in the perovskite layer and achieved a power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 20.1% and improved stability under ambient conditions.
  • Streamlined the data analysis process of photovoltaic device characteristics and performance.
