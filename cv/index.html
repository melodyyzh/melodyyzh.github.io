Research Experience

  • 2022 - present
    Graduate Student Research Assistant
    Lindsey Group & Glotzer Group, University of Michigan
    • Extended the machine-learned interaction model (ML-IAM), ChIMES, to describe how effective interactions between polymer-grafted nanoparticles depend on key physicochemical attributes, and integrated the model with digital alchemy inverse design for efficient optimization of particle attributes towards target self-assembled structures.
    • Designed a generalized ML-IAM framework that models anisotropic interactions for nanoparticles with convex shapes, enabling broad applicability to nanomaterial systems.
    • Developed an adaptive parallel meshing algorithm for training data generation that reduces required data volume by ~100x by focusing on key features of the target potential energy surface.
    • Contributed to the development and improvement of state-of-the-art machine-learning and simulation software, including ChIMES, HOOMD-Blue, and Freud.
  • 2020 - 2022
    Undergraduate Research Assistant
    Dou Group, Purdue University
    • Modified the surface of formamidinium-based perovskites using synthesized ligands to passivate defects and kinetically trap the photovoltaic crystal phase.
    • Incorporated amine-thiol chemistry in the perovskite layer and achieved a power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 20.1% and improved stability under ambient conditions.
    • Streamlined the data analysis process of photovoltaic device characteristics and performance.
