This summer, I started as an intern at [Micron Technology](https://www.micron.com/) on the **Advanced Modeling and AI Solutions (AMAIS)** team in Boise, ID.

I'm excited to bring my background in AI for scientific computing into an industry setting, working on problems where modeling, data, and engineering meet at semiconductor scale.

{% include figure.liquid path="assets/img/micron.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" zoomable=true caption="Micron Technology — Intelligence Accelerated" %}

My project focuses on building a material agent that interfaces multiple simulation and data analysis tools at Micron, providing a more robust and automated workflow.

I'm grateful for the opportunity to learn from and work alongside the engineers at Micron, and I'm excited for what this summer will bring.
