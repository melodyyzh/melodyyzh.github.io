I’m excited to share a recent [*MRS Bulletin* Materials News](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1557/s43577-026-01171-2) article I coauthored with [Ilya Mazalov](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ilya-mazalov/), “[Graph theory reveals how disorder enhances optical response in nanoparticle assemblies](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1557/s43577-026-01171-2).” In the piece, we highlight how graph-based analysis can help reveal the relationship between structural disorder and optical behavior in nanoparticle assemblies, offering a different way to understand and potentially design complex nanomaterials.
