I’m excited to share a recent [*MRS Bulletin* Materials News](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1557/s43577-026-01205-9) article I coauthored with [Ilya Mazalov](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ilya-mazalov/), “[Turning industrial nanoparticle furnace into a high-entropy alloy factory](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1557/s43577-026-01205-9).” The piece highlights how separating the flame from the reaction zone can turn an industrial nanoparticle furnace into a high-throughput route for supported high-entropy alloy nanoparticles, including alloys grown directly on supports from nanocrystals to metal–organic frameworks.
