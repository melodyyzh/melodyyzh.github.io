I’m excited to share a recent [*MRS Bulletin* Materials News](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1557/s43577-026-01170-3) article I coauthored with [Ilya Mazalov](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ilya-mazalov/), “[Shape-shifting nanoparticles introduce dynamic path for CO₂ conversion](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1557/s43577-026-01170-3).” The piece highlights how dynamically restructuring nanoparticles can open new pathways for catalytic CO₂ conversion, showing how changes in particle structure can directly influence reaction behavior.
