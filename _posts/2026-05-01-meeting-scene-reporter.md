I was excited to serve as a Meeting Scene Reporter at the Materials Research Society (MRS) Spring 2026 Meeting, highlighting symposium talks and making emerging materials research more accessible to a broader audience.

Pieces I covered:

1. [AI-Driven Integration of Molecular Modeling and Experimental Data](https://mrsmeetingscene.substack.com/p/symposium-mt04-from-atoms-to-algorithmsmachine). *MRS Meeting Scene*, Symposium MT04, 2026.

2. [Accelerating Battery Materials Discovery with AI- and Physics-Powered Molecular Universe Platform](https://mrsmeetingscene.substack.com/p/symposium-mt02-automated-materials). *MRS Meeting Scene*, Symposium MT02, 2026.

3. [Solvation Structure Modulation in Aqueous Electrolytes: Stabilizing Pyrazine Radicals and Facilitating Electrochemical Nitrate Reduction](https://mrsmeetingscene.substack.com/p/symposium-mt04-from-atoms-to-algorithmsmachine-1cd). *MRS Meeting Scene*, Symposium MT04, 2026.

4. Bespoke Machine-Learned Interatomic Potentials — Pushing the Limits of Accuracy and Efficiency. *MRS Meeting Scene*, Symposium MT03, 2026.

5. Optimization Method of Monomer Composites' Ratio Leading to the Best Polymer Property by Using Machine Learning Method and Quasi-Quantum Computing Method. *MRS Meeting Scene*, Symposium MT04, 2026.

It was a great opportunity to engage with emerging research across materials science and communicate it to a broader audience.
